Three ways to run

Same machines, same images, different guarantees.

On demand

Yours until you stop it

A dedicated GPU with root in a container or a full VM. Good for training runs you do not want to checkpoint around.

Interruptible

Half price, can be reclaimed

Bid for idle capacity. You get a signal before the machine goes; your volume stays and the job resumes on the next one.

Endpoints

Autoscaled inference

Give us an image and a port. We keep between zero and N replicas warm and bill only for the seconds they run.
